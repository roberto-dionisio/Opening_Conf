SLIDE 3 - THREE COMMUNITIES, ONE SET OF QUESTIONS                        [1:43]
===============================================================================

Let me say why we thought this particular meeting was worth organising.

There are three communities in this room, and each one arrives with something
the other two do not have.

Lattice field theory brings four decades of non-perturbative precision.
First-principles calculations, at the per-mille level, in a theory nobody can
solve with a pencil.

Machine learning brings new ways to sample, and new ways to represent what we
sample. Some of them are only a few years old, and they are already changing
what is affordable.

And quantum simulation brings access to regimes the Euclidean approach simply
cannot reach. Real time. Finite density. Strongly entangled states.

Three different sets of tools. But look at what they are all pointed at. It is
the same physics, and it has been the same physics all along.

    -> CLICK

One thing I want to be clear about, because it shaped the whole programme.
None of these three is a service department for the other two. They are three
genuine routes to the same questions, and those questions are older than any
of our methods.

That is why we did not run three parallel sessions. For three days, one room.
